Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how ONE WALLET ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information in connection with the ONE WALLET mobile application and related services (collectively, the "App"). It applies to all users of the App worldwide.

ONE WALLET is built on a privacy-first, minimal-collection philosophy. Because the App is a non-custodial multi-chain wallet, we have intentionally designed our systems so that the most sensitive information — your private keys, seed phrases, passwords, and PINs — never leaves your device. We never see it, store it, or transmit it.

This Policy forms part of, and is incorporated into, the ONE WALLET Terms of Use. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, you must not use the App.

3. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, and improve the App.

3.1 Device and Technical Information

When you use the App, we automatically collect limited technical information, including:

  • Device type, model, manufacturer, and operating system version;
  • Approximate installation language and locale;
  • Approximate IP address (used to infer general region for security and compliance);
  • App version, crash signals, and performance diagnostics;
  • Anonymized device identifiers (for fraud prevention and anti-abuse controls on the mining program);
  • Approximate session timestamps and event counters.

3.2 Public Wallet Addresses

To deliver mining rewards, display balances, and surface transaction history, we collect and associate public wallet addresses that you generate or import within the App. These addresses are, by the design of every supported blockchain, publicly visible on-chain to anyone — we do not treat them as confidential, and we do not link them to your real-world identity because we do not collect your identity.

3.3 Support Communications

If you voluntarily contact us (for example, by email), we will receive whatever information you choose to share, including your email address and the contents of your message. Please do not send us your seed phrase, private keys, or PIN — we will never ask for them, and legitimate support will never require them.

4. What We Do NOT Collect

To remove any ambiguity, ONE WALLET does not collect, request, store, or otherwise process:

  • Your private keys, seed phrases, mnemonic phrases, or recovery words — these remain exclusively on your device;
  • Your PIN, password, or biometric data — biometric unlock is handled locally by your device's operating system;
  • Your legal name, date of birth, government ID, passport, driver's licence, tax number, or KYC documents;
  • Your home address, phone number, or precise GPS coordinates;
  • Your banking, card, or bank-statement information;
  • Your social media accounts (unless you voluntarily and explicitly connect one for a specific feature);
  • Any information about your contacts, photos, calendar, or microphone beyond what is strictly required for an explicitly consented feature.

Because we do not collect this information, we cannot disclose it — to anyone, under any circumstances.

5. How We Use Information

We use the limited information we collect solely for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Providing the Service: delivering the App's features, showing your balances, routing swaps, and crediting mining rewards to the correct public wallet address;
  • Security and Anti-Abuse: detecting and preventing fraud, bot activity, sybil attacks, mining abuse, and unauthorized access;
  • Compliance and Eligibility Checks: enforcing geographic restrictions and sanctions screening as required under the Terms of Use;
  • Performance and Stability: diagnosing crashes, measuring latency, and improving the reliability of the App;
  • Product Improvement: understanding which features are used and how, in aggregate, to prioritize future development;
  • Communications: responding to your support inquiries and, where permitted, sending important service notices.

We do not use the information we collect to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell your information to third parties.

6. Public Blockchain Disclosure

You acknowledge and understand that public blockchains are transparent by design. Every transaction you make — including the sending address, the receiving address, the asset, and the amount — is permanently recorded on-chain and visible to anyone in the world, regardless of any action taken by ONE WALLET. This is a property of the underlying blockchain networks and is outside our control.

Please assume that anything you do on-chain is public, immutable, and potentially linkable to any other on-chain activity associated with the same address.

7. How We Share Information

We share information only in the narrow circumstances described below:

7.1 Service Providers. We may share limited technical information (such as device diagnostics, crash logs, or anonymized usage metrics) with trusted service providers — for example, cloud hosting, error reporting, and analytics vendors — acting under confidentiality and data-protection obligations and only to perform services on our behalf.

7.2 Third-Party Protocols. When you initiate an on-chain action such as a swap or bridge, the relevant transaction data (including your public address, amounts, and routing information) is transmitted to the applicable third-party decentralized protocol and recorded on the relevant blockchain. These third parties are independent of ONE WALLET and their handling of data is governed by their own terms and policies.

7.3 Legal and Safety Requirements. We may disclose information where we reasonably believe disclosure is required by a valid legal process (subpoena, court order, or equivalent), to comply with sanctions or regulatory obligations, to investigate suspected violations of our Terms, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of ONE WALLET, our users, or the public. Where legally permitted, we will notify the affected user before disclosure.

7.4 Corporate Transactions. If ONE WALLET is involved in a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or asset sale, information may be transferred to a successor entity subject to this Policy.

7.5 No Sale of Personal Information. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties for their independent use.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as is reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, to comply with applicable legal obligations, to enforce our Terms, and to resolve disputes. Operational logs (such as device diagnostics and security events) are typically retained for a limited period and are then deleted or irreversibly anonymized.

Because on-chain data (including transactions associated with any public wallet address you use) is recorded by the relevant blockchain network, that data cannot be deleted by ONE WALLET and will remain on-chain indefinitely.

9. Security

We implement administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect the information we collect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include transport encryption (TLS), access controls, secure software-development practices, and periodic review of our systems.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is perfectly secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, keeping your operating system up to date, and — above all — safeguarding your seed phrase and PIN. A compromised seed phrase gives the holder full and irreversible control of your wallet. Never share it with anyone, including anyone claiming to represent ONE WALLET.
